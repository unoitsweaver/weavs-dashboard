The logo

The shield mark — a navy shield with a white cross and a white hard hat, and the DSG wordmark — is a faithful approximation of the logo on the business card, redrawn as original SVG. No stock photography is used. The design uses the navy, sky-blue and gold palette of the cards, and original graphics, so the site is clearly the DSG brand and not a generic construction template.
