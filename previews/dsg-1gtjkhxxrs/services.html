Services

How we can help

From day-to-day compliance to construction-site inspections, the aim is the same: clear advice you can actually use, and arrangements that stand up when they are looked at.

Compliance

Most organisations already know they have health and safety duties. The difficulty is turning those duties into arrangements people will actually use. We review what you already have in place, identify gaps against the law and good practice, and help you put clear, proportionate arrangements in writing.

Fire Safety

We can help you assess fire risks, review your precautions, and keep the records a responsible person is expected to hold — so fire safety is thought through, not left as a once-a-year paper exercise.

Risk Assessment

A risk assessment should identify what could cause harm, who might be affected, and what should be done to reduce the risk so far as is reasonably practicable. The result ought to be a document a supervisor can follow, not a file that only a consultant can read. That is the standard we work to.

We provide health and safety training for your workplace. The right course depends on your team and the work you do — get in touch and we can talk it through.

Accreditations

Many businesses are asked for third-party health and safety accreditation when they tender. We can help you gather the right evidence, shape policies and records so they answer the scheme’s questions, and guide you through a submission.

Workplace Inspections

A workplace inspection is a structured look at conditions, behaviours and arrangements, with findings written down so they can be put right. The value is in the clarity of the report, and in following up what was found — not in a long checklist for its own sake.

Construction Site Inspections

Construction sites change quickly, and the risks change with them. A site inspection records what was seen on the day, what needs attention, and who should act — giving you, or your contractor, an independent view of how the site is being run.
