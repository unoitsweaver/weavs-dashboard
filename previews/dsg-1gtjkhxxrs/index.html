CDM, in plain English

What the Principal Designer actually does.

If you are having construction or refurbishment work done, the CDM Regulations place legal duties on you and on the people you appoint. Many clients are unsure what the Principal Designer role involves, or where it starts and ends.

Daniel holds CMaPS — Certified Membership of the Association for Project Safety — and we have set out the duties in plain English, so a client, a designer or a contractor can follow them.
